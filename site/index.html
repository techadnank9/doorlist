Questions.

Does Doorlist buy tickets?

No. It books free events and free tiers by itself. For anything with a price it stops and shows you the page so you can decide.

Do I need accounts on every site?

For Luma you sign in once inside Doorlist. Partiful, Eventbrite, Cerebral Valley and others ask you to sign in the first time you book on them, on their own pages, and remember you after that.

Is Doorlist part of Luma, Partiful or Eventbrite?

No. Doorlist is independent and books with your own accounts, the same way you would by hand.

What if an event asks something new?

Doorlist asks you once, by text or voice, and remembers the answer. If you're not in the app, you get a notification.
